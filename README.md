## 🤖 AgentCon 2025 – Microsoft Hyderabad

📅 *Date:* 11th October 2025  
🏢 *Venue:* Microsoft Office, Hyderabad  

On 11th October 2025, I had the opportunity to attend *AgentCon 2025* at Microsoft Office, Hyderabad.  
The event included sessions on *AI agents, **multi-agent orchestration, **AI in production, and tools like **LangChain, **Azure AI, and **n8n*.

### ✨ Highlights
- Agents of Tomorrow: Building the Next Generation of Intelligence – *Henk Boelman*  
- Building Production-Ready AI Agents with LangChain – *Viswanatha Swamy*  
- Voice AI Agents & Smart Expense-Tracking AI Agents – *Harsh Gupta & Stephen Simon*  
- Hands-on Labs: Building autonomous support agents and extending AI capabilities  
- AI-Powered Development: Accelerating coding and orchestrating AI agents using advanced protocols  

I felt inspired to explore *AI agent development and orchestration* for future projects.
